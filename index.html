Which columns do I need?

Orders need order_id, order_date, currency, amount, status. Customer and items are optional, and they unlock the customer and product views. Only paid orders count as revenue.

Refunds need refund_id, order_id, refund_date, currency, amount. Reason is optional. If there were no refunds, load a file with just the header row.

Dates use YYYY-MM-DD, amounts are plain numbers such as 62.95, and the currency is USD. Each file can be up to 5 MB and 100,000 rows.
